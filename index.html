About

Bad Karl is a local 8-piece funk and soul band out of Columbus, Ohio, featuring high energy rhythmic songs from the 60s through today. Inspired by the timeless grooves of James Brown to Stevie Wonder and Sly & Family Stone, Bad Karl thrives on unique arrangements and expanded improvisation.

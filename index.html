About

Bad Karl is a local 8-piece funk and soul band out of Columbus, Ohio, playing high-energy, rhythmic songs from the 60s through today. Inspired by everything from the timeless grooves of James Brown and Stevie Wonder to the modern funk of Bruno Mars and Vulfpeck, Bad Karl thrives on unique arrangements and expanded improvisation.
